In an attempt to make college more affordable, Massachusetts is approving 3-year degrees. The [full story](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/02/12/massachusetts-will-allow-3-year-degree-proposals) is covered in Inside Higher Ed. Massachusetts already provides free tuition for community colleges. This begs the question: is there a path to earning a full degree, but only paying 1 year's worth of tuition?
